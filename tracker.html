HYDRA — Progress

HYDRA is a decentralized protocol that maintains a probabilistic representation of world state — past, present, and projected — assembled from the contributions of many independent sources. It is not a price feed, not a prediction market, not a database.

Its defining commitment is honesty about uncertainty: distributions, not point estimates; genuine disagreement preserved, not averaged away; reputation tracking calibration, not accuracy. Permissionless, immutable from genesis, adversarially-robust by composition rather than gatekeeping.

This tracker measures architectural progress against the vision. Percentages reflect alignment + readiness, not feature completeness against an end-state.
