HYDRA — a probabilistic oracle of world state

HYDRA is a decentralized protocol that maintains a probabilistic representation of world state — past, present, and projected — assembled from the contributions of many independent sources.

Its defining commitment is honesty about uncertainty: distributions, not point estimates; genuine disagreement preserved, not averaged away; reputation tracking calibration, not accuracy. Permissionless, immutable from genesis, adversarially-robust by composition rather than gatekeeping.

This tracker measures architectural progress against the vision. Percentages reflect alignment + readiness, not feature-completeness.

Overall progress weighs in test confidence. Each module's bar shows its raw build progress; the overall figure folds in the latest /hydra-test confidence per module — strong-confidence modules (≥80) get full credit, moderate (60–79) take a 15% discount, weak or untested work takes a 30% discount.
